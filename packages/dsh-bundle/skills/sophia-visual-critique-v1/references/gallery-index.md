# Anti-pattern gallery: index

The gallery is fourteen page images in Chinese: 51 specimens cropped from real delivered artifacts (names blurred), each group opening with a green line that states the legitimate form and closing with a green "instead" frame of real-world benchmarks (sources credited). The rules on page 1 [I0.1]:

- These elements are recurring "default moves" unrelated to the subject. While composing and at the final check, compare page by page: when your own page grows the same element, stop and restructure; do not cover it with decoration.
- The disease is in the format and the default, not in the capability: each group's green opening line names the legitimate form, and its closing green frame shows the real-world "instead".
- Benchmarks teach method and judgement of form; never copy their brand elements, palettes or layouts. A hit does not mean deleting everything of that kind: first ask what job the element does for the reader; if there is no answer, delete it.
