# Positive precedents: index

The precedent pages are Chinese page images. Page 1 states the rule: **precedents are a yardstick and vocabulary, not templates. Priority is always: the task's world > the person's and the references' constraints > precedents.** [Q0.1]

**How to use (page 1).** Before deciding a direction, read page 1 and the page for your artifact type. Each precedent has four columns: *what it faced* (its task), *key decision* (what it dared and why it holds), *how to transfer* (an abstract judgement usable across subjects) and *what not to copy* (surface features: copying them makes the next template disease). When direction candidates are needed, take their anchors from different style lineages of the page. Before delivery, set your artifact beside the chosen precedent and write the three things it still does better. [Q0.2]

**One iron rule (page 1).** What precedents share is unfakeable reality (live numbers, real logos, real products, operable evidence) and the strength of deletion (every drop of ink has a job). When a precedent seems to conflict with the gallery, the gallery wins: precedents teach how good can be, the gallery guards the floor. [Q0.3]
