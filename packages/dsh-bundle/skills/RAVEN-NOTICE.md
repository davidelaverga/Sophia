The reference images under `sophia-visual-critique-v1/references/gallery/` (the donor's `review-against-ai-patterns/references/anti-slop-gallery/page-1.jpg` … `page-14.jpg`) and `sophia-web-finish-v1/references/precedents/` (the donor's `build-polished-visual-frontends/references/design-precedents/page-1.jpg`, `page-4.jpg`, `page-6.jpg`) are the donor's bytes, unmodified, each identified in `manifest.json` by its Git blob and SHA-256. Some show crops or screenshots of third parties' published work. Apache-2.0 covers what the donor licensed; it does not by itself establish redistribution rights for every third-party specimen. Each image is marked `rights: pending_review` until Codex's asset review (SDD-01 binding map, decision O-2). Nothing here is installed or executed from Raven.

The prompts in `prompts/` are the Sophia Native Design Mission Pack v0.1's `runtime/` candidates, byte for byte.
