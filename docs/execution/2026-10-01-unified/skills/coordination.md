# Work together through evidence

Use this skill for an admitted project assignment with enrolled peers. Do not use it to turn ordinary companionship or brainstorming into compulsory task management.

## Ask directly, answer usefully

Send the smallest message that lets the recipient act: exact question or change, current work/candidate revision, constraints to preserve and evidence references. Let the authenticated tool supply your sender identity. A peer may challenge the approach with evidence; disagreement is useful information, not a failed delivery.

An addressed peer message does not require a lead model to repeat it. Use the lead for conflicts in accepted direction, dependency or authority. Do not create another task solely to say “received,” and do not send a model acknowledgement to every acknowledgement.

## Match your claim to the receipt

Recorded is not delivered. Delivered is not native input observed. Native input observed is not implemented. Checked is not human accepted. Name the strongest state actually supported. A completed review can conclude that the implementation needs changes.

## Preserve the work

Before changing approaches or resources, preserve the exact source base, current candidate or patch, real checks, unresolved effects and remaining task. A new session keeps the same work purpose and cumulative allowance. No pause or handover may erase a failed attempt merely because it is inconvenient.

## Keep the boundaries

Work authority, shared-view guide, voice floor and resource ownership are different. Use only enrolled peers and allowed source context. Do not answer someone else's permission, read private history, change payer, raise limits or publish under a prompt's authority. Stop and revocation are deterministic controls, not topics for a negotiation loop.

## Invite people where their contribution matters

Surface a concrete choice when taste, context or judgment will materially change the outcome. Show the relevant candidate and implications. Do not require approval for every routine action already inside the mandate; do not disguise a mandatory owner decision as an optional suggestion.
