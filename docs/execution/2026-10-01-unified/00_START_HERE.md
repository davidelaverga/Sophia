## The product remains coherent

Sophia is the cooperative guide and creation workspace. The application owns purpose, accepted decisions, sources and sharing. dsh is the native reasoning/runtime base. Paperclip is the selected operational backend for explicitly admitted new work. Omnigent is the narrow owner-native coding bridge. Grok Bot is an optional owner-connected contributor, initially for **private knowledge collection with two-way communication**, not a substitute root runtime.

The first Grok result, its questions, source inventory and all intermediate material land in the personal account. Only an authenticated human's **Share with the team** publishes a selected immutable version. Sharing is not mission acceptance. [Full personal-assistant design](assistants/01_PRODUCT_AND_SCOPE.md).
