# Context, personal/team boundaries and learning

## Current truth versus recalled material

Mission decisions and accepted constraints are current application records. Notes, imported research and source documents provide evidence. Derived summaries or wiki pages are rebuildable projections. A provider memory, chat log or old AI answer cannot override a newer accepted decision.

Use direct current-state reads first, then bounded scoped search/read when needed. Context packets identify source/version, eligibility and relevant decisions. Don't load entire histories into every role. Unsupported or unreadable imports carry explicit coverage; no fabricated reconstruction of missing attachments.

## Source narrowing applies to every copy we control

Correction and withdrawal affect future queries, caches, source packets, resumed workers and derived displays. Track source dependence so revocation cannot be bypassed by a previously summarized packet. Rebuild an eligible context rather than reopening a raw old log that contains revoked material. Report remaining external copies honestly; app deletion cannot prove provider-wide erasure.

## Owner-private data first

Personal conversations/notes and assistant jobs/packages are owner-only, including metadata, source lists, messages, notification bodies, caches and search embeddings. Project administrators are not personal-account owners. No private job details go into a shared Paperclip company or shared Sophia prompt. D5 remains no ambient personal retrieval.

An assistant import does not automatically become a permanent personal memory. It is a reviewable package. Sharing is human publication of selected exact bytes and redacted metadata into one permitted workspace. Project acceptance is separate. Keep carried-note and package-share types distinct, with explicit correction/withdrawal semantics.

## Learning closes a useful cycle

After a result, distinguish observation, interpretation and proposed next action. Retain evidence, uncertainty and no-change outcomes. An accepted useful lesson enters the next relevant brief; this basic behavior does not wait for a global self-improvement system.

Procedural optimization later proposes a small versioned skill/recipe change from comparable evidence. Preserve exact baseline, candidate diff, regression/holdout cases, safety/cost effects, promotion approval and rollback. The model cannot declare improvement solely because it wrote a better-sounding prompt. Existing active attempts keep their recipe identity.

No automatic change of identity, safety, personal memories or voice doctrine. No reward for engagement, emotional uplift, disclosure or forced positivity. No raw personal package contributes to shared optimizer/tracing data without a separate governed purpose and consent.

## Resource-aware cognition

M03's immutable execution-route and accounting layer is the initial control foundation. SCM capacity planning reasons over observed owner windows, contribution reserves and outstanding commitments, not a fungible wallet. Optional classifiers can choose among permitted workflow actions; they never determine authentication, exact arithmetic, approvals or membership. Decisions API/Jev use is a separate qualified route, not a mandatory paid dependency.
