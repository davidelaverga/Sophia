## Acceptance and adverse cases

- `S2-04-T01` — The correct owner resolves one supported request from mobile and its native resolution is observed.
- `S2-04-T02` — An unsupported native request remains visible with an accurate handoff.
- `S2-04-T03` — A mobile steer reaches the currently authorized worker through the lead.
- `S2-04-T04` — A stale tap after Stop cannot grant a superseded operation.
- `S2-04-T05` — A member cannot answer a different owner request by reusing its URL.
- `S2-04-T06` — A lost native response is not silently retried as a fresh grant.
