# Decisions, supersession and conflict resolution

## Effective planning hierarchy

1. Current owner decisions and repository security/effect rules remain controlling.
2. Actual source and dated evidence determine what exists; they do not authorize new scope.
3. This v2.0 pack is the cumulative forward plan, including the explicitly retained contracts and current in-flight continuation.
4. Later approved feature changes identify the exact clause, contract and goal they amend.
5. Previous packs and older alternative architectures are history/evidence only.

Do not use "newest file wins" to replace source permissions or to treat a PR author's statement as a fresh direct owner approval. Installation changes navigation, not runtime. An in-flight mission keeps its author, issue, allowance, active attempt and current controller.

**SCM-IDENTITY:** use the management pack's company-scoped Sophia integration principal, distinct from the human initiator carried in an authenticated envelope. Do not revert to earlier unqualified user-ID impersonation or an admin credential plus a claimed actor.

**SOURCE/PROVIDER RIGHTS:** owner access is not automatic permission to export employer/client data. Qualification records permitted uses and actual data handling. No legal clearance or commercial reseller status is inferred.
