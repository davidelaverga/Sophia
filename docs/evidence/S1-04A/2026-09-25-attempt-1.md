# S1-04A — Room access, attempt 1 (2026-09-25)

**Goal** (added by Luis, outside the v0.4 pack, which names membership invitation as product work):
bring people into a project's room by QR code or an emailed invitation, have them wait in a lobby until
someone inside lets them in, and put the room's sessions on a calendar.
**Level:** `sql-run` plus browser (real Studio, API, PostgreSQL 16, local LiveKit server; synthetic HS256 identities, including an anonymous guest).
**Owner:** Luis · **Branch:** `rooms/access` (on `studio/vision`) · **Contract:** amendment A02 · **Migration:** 0010.

## Decisions (Luis, 2026-09-25)

- **"Queue" means a lobby.** Whoever arrives by QR or link waits until an editor or admin lets them in. A photo of the QR code is not enough to enter.
- **Two kinds of invitation.**
  - **Guest:** no account. The guest gives a name, waits, and reaches only the call.
  - **Member:** bound to one email. It adds the person as an editor or viewer.
- **Sophia sends the email.** Resend in production. Locally, the email is written to files (`.sophia-mail/`), and nothing is sent.

## What was built

1. **Migration 0010.** Room sessions, invitations and the lobby, each with RLS (invitation rows: editors and admins only).
   - Every write is a `sophia.*` function that re-checks authority and emits one project event.
   - Guest invitations: editors and admins. Member invitations: admins only.
   - Knock, admit and deny; one lobby entry per person and room. A member who knocks is admitted at once; a denied guest stays denied.
   - Acceptance only by the invited email. Accepting moves the audience revision.
   - Sessions end within a day of their start, in the future.
2. **Links.**
   - A token is HMAC-SHA256(server secret, invitation id and version) in base64url. Only its SHA-256 is stored.
   - The link is `STUDIO_URL/join#token`: the token rides in the fragment, which no server or log sees, and reaches the API only in request bodies.
   - "New link" is a compare-and-set on the version: the old link stops working.
3. **API** (contract amendment A02, 12 operations).
   - The invitation preview is public, and an unknown token reveals nothing.
   - An anonymous Supabase sign-in (`is_anonymous`) may only knock, read its own entry and get a token for the call. Everything else answers 403.
   - A guest's LiveKit token carries `{"guest":true}` and a member's carries their role. The API signs this metadata, and a participant cannot change it.
   - Denying an admitted guest takes them out of the call (LiveKit RoomService).
   - Emails are sent once per link version (the provider's idempotency key). A session adds an `.ics` file. Logs hold the invitation id, never the address or the link.
4. **Studio.**
   - **Invite sheet:** the room link with its QR; send a guest their own link; member invitations; the calendar; guests in the room, with Remove.
   - **Lobby card on the stage:** Let in / Not now, for editors and admins.
   - **Next session:** shown under Sophia's line.
   - **`/join` page:** the preview, then the guest's name, the wait and the call, or the member's sign-in and acceptance.
   - Guests and viewers are never offered the floor.
