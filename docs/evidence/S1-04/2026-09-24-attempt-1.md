## What was built

1. **Shell and routes.** `/p/<project>/<view>` for Studio, Goals, Work, Knowledge, Updates and Resources. Links from before S1-04 (`/projects/<id>`) still open. Views whose records come later say which goal brings them, instead of showing fake content.
2. **Viewer-local state.** Lens (Converse, Explore, Build) and unsent drafts are kept per viewer and project on the device and never sent to the server (`viewer-state.ts`).
3. **Room contract (amendment A01) and migration 0009.**
   - Every project has a room: backfilled for existing projects, and a trigger on project insert.
   - The snapshot exposes the room.
   - `transferInputFloor` is a compare-and-set on the room revision, idempotent per actor and key, and emits one `room.input_floor_changed` event.
   - Taking the floor: anyone while it is free, the holder, or an admin (reclaim). Only admins and editors can receive it; viewers listen.
4. **Room tokens.** `issueRoomToken` checks membership, that the room belongs to the project, and the audience revision. It returns a LiveKit token:
   - identity: the verified actor;
   - name: the email from the verified token, never from the client;
   - grant: one room only;
   - ttl: 600 s;
   - publishing: microphone only, and never for viewers.
5. **Dock.**
   - Join and leave; the people in the room with their speaking and muted state; mute.
   - The input floor: take, pass, and reclaim from someone who left the room.
   - An honest line that Sophia's voice arrives with S1-05.
   - Remote voices play through hidden audio elements. `livekit-client` loads only on join.
   - On phones the dock folds: status, Mute and Leave stay visible.
