## Method and limits

Sixteen frames were sampled across each recording, with closer inspection of selected frames. The original clips are low-resolution screen recordings, not mobile-screen acceptance tests. The review did not assess their audio, transcribe narration, or measure interactive latency. No screenshot establishes a server-side permission, model invocation or completed work effect.

The source filenames and SHA-256 values are in [video-source-register.json](video-source-register.json). Full contact sheets are deliberately not included in the portable/public-ready packet because the invitation demonstration includes a QR code and link; do not publish those tokens or reuse them.
