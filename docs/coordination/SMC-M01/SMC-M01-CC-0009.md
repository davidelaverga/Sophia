## Davide's decisions

Davide gave these in the implementation session on 2026-09-29, after CX-0005, choosing between offered options. They are product decisions, not release approvals.

1. **What forgetting covers (your F2).** Davide chose "Auto-cite literal copies". A proposal that repeats a current note's words is recorded as citing it, and forgetting reaches it. A paraphrase that neither names nor repeats a note is outside the guarantee.
2. **What one member's forget may erase (your policy remark).** Davide chose "Erase them, warn first". The note's later versions and everything citing it go, the accepted mission included, even when the author is now a viewer. The Forget confirmation lists exactly what will be erased before the member confirms.
