## 1. Why a revision

After CC-0013, CI's `runtime-unit` job failed on `750c3ff`, a docs-only commit, in two media-bridge tests:
- **The cause.** A retry test gave a failed read's two retries 5 ms of real time before checking its answer. On a busy runner the second retry was still pending. Its fake also pushed into the module-level service, so the late call was counted by the next test.
- **The fix, `126cea3`.** Both tests wait, bounded, for the answer, and each fake pushes into its own test's service. It changes `apps/media-bridge/src/room-session.test.ts` only; no source file of any deployed process changes. Under CPU load, 1 of 15 runs failed before the fix; after it, 0 of 30 runs of the two tests and 0 of 12 runs of the whole file failed.

This moved PR #18's head with a non-docs change, which revision 1's precondition "later commits change only `docs/`" does not allow. Hence revision 2, bound to the new head, as Davide's instruction asked ("the latest commit").
