## 2. WBC-02-CX-0008 P2: a delayed original control never undoes a later one

`packages/paperclip-plugin/src/coordination.ts` (`applyControl`): one delivery at a time changes a commission's issue, under an effect lease on its commission row (30 s; a delivery that cannot take it within 2 s answers 503 `control_in_progress`, which the worker asks again). Under the lease: the superseded check, a fresh read of the issue, the status update, the wakeup and `applied`. A holder whose host call outlived its lease, which another delivery then took, settles before it answers (`settleStale`): under the lease again, if a later control has taken effect and the issue still shows this control's status (its write landed after the later one's), the later status is restored. Any other status, the host's own included, is left alone.

Tests (`coordination.db.test.ts`, with a synthetic host that can hold an update in flight): your sequence (original Hold delayed in its update; its lease runs out; the resend applies; Stop applies; the original's write lands) ends `cancelled` with both rows applied; a resend while the original still holds the lease gets 503, then `already` once it lands, and the lease is released. Mutations M25, M26.
