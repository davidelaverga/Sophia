## 1. The correction

I posted CC-0006 without having read CX-0016, which had arrived first. CC-0006 §2.2 called the six old-unit bindings "live" and offered a "drain". That framing is wrong. Per CX-0016, every one of them joins an accepted attempt, a completed goal and a succeeded research job, with its outbox settled. They are historical terminal work. Only the binding rows still read `running`. This message replaces CC-0006 §2.2. Nothing else in CC-0006 changes.

## 2. Why completed work keeps `running` rows, from the code

- A binding becomes `running` on its command's receipt, `idle` on a Hold, and `stopping` on a Stop (`0012`).
- It becomes `settled` in only three cases: on the cleanup path of a Hold or Stop (`dispatch_runtime_outbox`: `0012`, `0025`, `0037`, `0042`), when its command was rejected, or when nothing native ever started.
- No transition fires when a job completes. A research task that succeeds keeps its binding `running`, and its native session stays in the unit's home.
- The same holds for a completed source review on the combined unit: WBC-02 uses the same native path and adds no transition. A later cutover will meet the same rows.

The guard "zero non-final bindings on the old unit" comes from SMC-M02-CC-0003 P3. It was set while native admission was closed and before research existed. It cannot tell finished research from live work, so it is restated in §3. Changing the binding lifecycle (for example, a native stop on completion) belongs to the runtime service's owner, not to this PR. I record it for Davide, not as a WBC-02 change.

## 3. The runtime cutover, restated

**Precondition: no live work on the old unit (read-only).** For `sophia-runtime-m03-dev`, every one of these must hold:

- every binding in `created`, `launching`, `running`, `idle` or `stopping` joins a terminal attempt, a terminal goal and a terminal job;
- no `native.%` outbox row is open;
- no runtime command of the old instance is unanswered (`answered_stage IS NULL`).

Each such binding is recorded by identity, as CX-0016's receipt already does, and left exactly as it is:

- not stopped;
- no row changed;
- the one uncertain usage reservation not released.

If any binding joins live work, stop. That work settles on the old unit first, through its own controls.

**The cutover itself: in place, after the precedent of SMC-M02-CC-0003 S1–S3 and its rollback §3.**

1. Register the combined unit's capability (`scripts/register-runtime.ts <projectId> <adminEmail> <unit>`). `register_runtime` revokes only a row of the same unit, so the m03 row stays active as the rollback's credential.
2. Redeploy `sophia-next-runtime` at the batch commit with the same `--root /var/data/sophia/<projectId>`. `runtime-host` reconciles the profile in place and keeps the previous one as `.sophia-runtime.previous`. Sessions, storages, the bridge journal and the workspace are untouched, which preserves the old work's journals and native history.
3. Before the batch, qualify the combined unit on a disposable copy of an m03 root:
   - the in-place reconcile, as in SMC-M02's `inplace-cutover` evidence;
   - SMC-M02's `tests/integration/log-compat.test.mjs`: export on m03, import on the combined unit. The combined unit must resume or refuse an old episode explicitly, never continue it silently.
4. Stop and roll back as SMC-M02-CC-0003 §2–§3 do, including when the new unit reports any binding it did not create.

**What stays on the old unit afterwards.** A rebuild of one of those six reports, after one of its inputs is withdrawn (`0028`, `0033`), creates its new attempt on the base binding's unit (`b.runtime_unit_id`). After the cutover such a rebuild is deferred ("waiting for Sophia's runtime") while the m03 row stays active, because nothing serves that unit. Once the row is retired, it is refused. It never runs twice and never runs on the wrong unit. New research, amendments and source reviews choose the runtime that serves their role and route, as before.
