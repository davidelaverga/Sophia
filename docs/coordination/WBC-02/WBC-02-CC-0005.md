## 5. Bounded pilot and model costs

From the route as the unit records it (`gpt-6-luna`/high under `openai-review`: $0.10 input, $0.01 cached input, $0.125 cache write, $0.50 output per million tokens; 16,000 output tokens at most; a 272,000-token window):

| Bound | Amount | How it is enforced |
|---|---|---|
| One model call, worst case (a full window written to cache, the whole output ceiling) | $0.042 | the bridge reserves its estimate before the call and settles the actual |
| One review, worst case (eight calls) | $0.336 | eight calls at most, refused beyond (`research_limit_reached`) |
| One review, as allowed | the allowance chosen at Start, at most `least(review cap, research task cap)` | Sophia refuses a reservation the allowance cannot hold |
| The pilot | D3: the number of reviews × the review cap (e.g. five reviews at a $0.50 cap: $2.50 at most) | the coordination grant's cap; disabling the grant stops new reviews |
| Estimate, not a bound | about $0.006 for a four-call review over 32 KiB of sources (one cache write, then cached reads) | none: the measured cost is reported per run |

Paperclip's hosting cost is Codex's quote (D4). No paid call happens before D1–D3 and Codex's INT-19 under the recorded allowance.

## 6. Rollback

- **New reviews:** `set_coordination_grant(<project>, 'disabled', …)`: proposals are refused, permits deny `not_enrolled`, the board stays readable. Removing the worker variables stops deliveries; pending ones wait.
- **A running review:** Hold or Stop from the board (fenced at once); a Paperclip cancel holds it too.
- **Runtime:** back to the previous unit, rebuilt from its own commit; the reviewer is unavailable, research untouched.
- **Code:** the previous API, worker and Studio run on `0042` unchanged; `0042` stays (no down migration) and its tables are idle without a grant.
- **Paperclip:** disable or uninstall the plugin and the adapter, or stop the service. Its database is separate and may be kept or dropped by D4's operator. Sophia's records stay authoritative; a commission whose delivery is unknown is reconciled by its key, never created twice.
- **A Paperclip issue under the host's recovery hold** after a genuinely failed run (CX-0009) is resolved by an operator in Paperclip; Sophia keeps the delivery unconfirmed meanwhile and never clears the hold.
