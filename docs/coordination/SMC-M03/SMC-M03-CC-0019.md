### Defaults chosen for Davide (each can change)

1. **Captions: who.** The same members who get chat packets today: signed members present, never guests. Only the floor holder's speech is transcribed, as today.
2. **Captions: what is kept.** Nothing. No API write, no log of caption text, no storage. Studio keeps captions in memory for the page session only. Captions are separate from the project's note policy and from structured notes.
3. **Captions: how they show.** Sophia's words show as they arrive, and a reply cut off by an interruption is marked as such. Spoken lines carry a small "spoken" cue. New captions do not mark Chat as new; screen readers get finished lines only.
4. **Captions: off switch.** The bridge's `SOPHIA_LIVE_CAPTIONS=off` turns them off without a Studio deploy. They are on by default.
5. **Cards: what is recorded.** `text_recipients` now counts distinct members who got the card in any mode. In a voice-only room, an announcement no one heard is recorded once members have the card, which is the rule mixed rooms already follow (RF-0017). Only a bridge restart without a handover loses the remaining spoken retries.
6. **Cards: Chat mark.** A voice member's Chat is marked new for a result card, including one they just heard, so they can find Open and Download. After a page reload, cards shown again may mark Chat once.
7. **Citations.** The prior version stays a cited source of an amendment (its lineage). The pilot's v2 record is left as it is.
