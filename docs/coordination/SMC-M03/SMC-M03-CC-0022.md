*What limits depth today:*
- **The worker never learns what "enough" means.** The frozen prompt says to stop at "sufficient supported coverage". The first message never defines it, and the `depth` preference is passed through without any meaning attached.
- **Reads stop at the first page.** A read returns its first 6,000 characters. Nothing says when to read further pages, although further pages are free.
- **The allowance runs out early.** It is 5 searches and 8 reads per lineage. CX-0027's v1 used all 5 searches, so its follow-up had none left.
- **Money is not the limit.** Model calls cost about $0.26–0.28 per task against a $5 cap.

*Proposed criteria.* All are measurable from stored versions, without spend.
- **Floors**:
  - D1, read-backed cited sources: at least 3, 5 or 7 for brief, standard or deep, capped at the reads actually settled.
  - D2: no citation of a search result.
  - D3: the share of claims with a citation (0.60, 0.75 or 0.80).
  - D4: every body section cites something.
  - D6: a substantive limitations section.
  - D7: sources come from more than one site.
  - D8: length inside its band, with limits stated in the question taking precedence.
  - D9: searches within their target, and one search and one read kept for a follow-up unless the task is deep.
- **Depth signals**:
  - G1, grounding: at least 0.8 of the numbers and dates in cited claims appear in a cited source's stored text.
  - G2: no claim rests only on a search snippet.
  - G3: reads went past the first page where the source is longer than one page.
  - D5, cross-checks: claims backed by two independent read sources, at least 1, 3 or 5.
- **Davide's own rubric** (R1–R5, 0–2 each) stays the acceptance gate. Each run is compared with the same question's earlier version.

*Proposed brief.* It goes in the worker's first message, built by `sophia.research_prompt` in SQL, which is not frozen. Fresh tasks only; amendments get the update brief from item 1.
> Depth: standard. If the question or the assumptions set a length, sections, or fewer searches or reads, follow them exactly; otherwise these targets define sufficient coverage. When the task is a synthesis of the given inputs, the inputs are the evidence: read each in full and do not search to reach these numbers.
> - Evidence: read at least 5 distinct sources and cite each one you rely on. A search result is a lead, not evidence.
> - Read past the first page: continue each source you rely on with research_read_context until the relevant passage is covered, up to four pages a source. Paging a stored source uses no search or read.
> - Spend reads, not searches: at most 3 searches, each for a different need (primary documentation; independent data; a contrary view); follow link: refs. Leave one search and one read for a later revision.
> - Cross-check at least 3 key claims with two independent sources; where they disagree, say which and why.
> - Cite next to each claim. The answer first; a section "What the evidence does not settle".
> - Length: about 900 to 1,800 words.

Brief and deep differ only in the numbers. The deep ceiling is about 2,400 words, so the report still fits one draft call.
