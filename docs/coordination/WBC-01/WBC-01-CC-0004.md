## What was wrong, and the fix

`SessionActs` offered Try again for any command whose admission was unknown. That covered the latest line and the Earlier list, whatever was allowed now, so a lost Stop could be sent again after its task stopped being observed or after Stop was denied to the viewer.

Now one rule, `retryableNow(k, sendable)` in `resources/receipts.ts`, decides both whether Try again is offered and whether the handler sends. A command is tried again only when both hold:
- its admission is unknown;
- its kind is among what may be sent here now. On a task, that is the view's per-viewer actions, and nothing while the task isn't observed (`actions.ts` `offered`). On a resource's row, it is the route's supported controls, for its owner only, as before.

Otherwise the line still says what is uncertain, without promising a retry: "Not confirmed whether it was recorded. It can't be sent again from here now; it is kept as it was." The command keeps its operation. Once it may be sent again, Try again returns and sends that same operation and payload.
