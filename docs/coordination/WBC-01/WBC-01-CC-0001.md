## What the Studio adopts, unchanged

1. **The view.** `sophia.work.board.v1` with its plans as `sophia.work.plan.v2`, field for field as the packet's schema. `next_checkpoint` and `decisions` belong to the goal's view, not the plan. A plan's revision changes only by a planning decision; activity, queueing and report age change the view.
2. **One guard, one conversion.** `readBoardView(unknown)` accepts a view or rejects it with reasons. It checks the schema's shape and its conditional rules (complete needs a satisfied policy with evidence; an accepted plan needs its decision; a candidate trigger needs its producer). The board's rows come from the accepted view through one function. The guard is feature-local until WBC-02 generates a validator in `@sophia/contracts`.
3. **Receipts.** `sophia.work.receipt.v1`. Any number of receipts can arrive, in any order, for one `operation_id`. The highest `revision` wins. Admission never goes back from `recorded`: a later `unknown` keeps it, and a later `rejected` turns delivery and effect to unknown. A settled effect (`held`, `stopped`, `resumed`, `choice_recorded`) is final. Sending is local and is never shown as a receipt.
4. **Placement.** A plan names the view's project and its own goal. A view whose plans name another is refused.

## What the Studio sends (ports only; nothing is bound to an endpoint here)

| Port | Request | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Command | `{operation_id, kind: guidance\|hold\|resume\|stop, text?, work_id, assignment_id, assignment_generation, attempt_id}`. A retry after an unknown outcome reuses `operation_id` and payload | receipts, as above |
| Decision | `{operation_id, decision_id, revision, choice, work_id, plan_id, plan_revision, candidate_version_ref}`. After an unknown outcome, only the same choice with the same `operation_id` | a `decision` receipt: `choice_recorded`, or rejected `conflict` / `denied` / `expired`, or `unknown` |
| Ask | `{question_id, work_id, plan_id, plan_revision, candidate_version_ref, text}` | events `{question_id, seq, kind: chunk\|complete\|unavailable\|failed, text?}`; WBC-02 shows it unavailable |
| Result | `{work_id, source_id, version_id, sha256, media_type}`, for `open_result` and `review_candidate` | the bytes of that exact version, or unavailable; the Studio never builds a provider URL |

## Four readings to confirm

1. **Unknown is not unassigned.** `lifecycle: unknown`, or an item whose plan names an assignee while its view has `assignment: null` and isn't `planned`, goes to **Active**, marked as not observed. It never goes to Unassigned, and it gets no write control.
2. **No success without evidence.** A view that says `complete` without a satisfied policy and evidence fails the guard. It is not shown as success, and it is not repaired.
3. **For you** is a pending `waiting_on` whose `respondent_id` is the viewer, a proposed decision of theirs, or work assigned to them by hand. Owning the account that runs the work doesn't count. The viewer comes from the signed-in identity, not from the view.
4. **Availability is only what the view says.** `available_actions` decides every shared task control. A missing entry means unavailable. Native permission answers and reserve changes are not task actions; they stay owner-only, in Resources.
