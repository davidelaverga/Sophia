## Personal's Correct and Forget: the pointer overlap, measured

The overlap was measured on the fixture page (`personal.html?memory=1&notes=open`, desktop 1280×800). Each memory row was hovered and left until no fade was running, then `document.elementFromPoint` was read at each button's centre:

| Memory | Its day (faded, `opacity: 0`) | Correct | Forget | A press at Forget's centre reached |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| m1 | "Yesterday", x 1161–1220, y 199–210 | x 1109–1171 | x 1173–1228, y 190–218 | the day's `span.c3-mem-at` |
| m2 | "Today", x 1187–1220 | x 1109–1171 | x 1173–1228 | the day |
| m3 | "Today", x 1187–1220 | x 1109–1171 | x 1173–1228 | the day |

Correct's centre reached Correct on every row, because these days are short. A memory learned in another year is said in full. With `memory=old`, m1's day reads "Aug 31, 2025", at x 1141–1220: it covers Correct from 34 px across.

**Why.** A memory's day and its actions share one grid cell (`.c3-mem-at` and `.c3-mem-acts`), and the day comes first. An element with opacity below 1 is painted as a layer of its own, above the row's other content:
- During the fade both are such layers, and the actions, coming later, are on top.
- Once the actions reach `opacity: 1` they stop being one. The day, at `opacity: 0`, is then drawn and hit-tested above them.

So once the 140 ms fade ends, a press there lands on the invisible day, and Forget does nothing. The check was flaky only because Playwright's click sometimes came during the fade. On a desktop, a person's press after the fade misses Forget at its centre every time. On a phone (`hover: none`), the actions sit on their own line, and nothing covers them.

**The fix:** `.places .c3-mem-at` gets `pointer-events: none`. The day is only read, never pressed, so it lets the press through. Nothing else changes: what shows, the fade, the hover and focus rules, the phone layout, the accessibility tree.

| Check | What it holds |
