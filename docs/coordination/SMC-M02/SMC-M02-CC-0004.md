## 3. The two running `s1-03-dev` bindings

**What they are.** A binding's state moves as follows:
1. `apply_runtime_receipt` (0012) moves it to `running` when its create is delivered.
2. `capture_native_result` (0016) then moves the job to `succeeded` and the goal to `checking`, and leaves the binding alone.
3. From then on, only a goal Hold (`idle`) or Stop (`settled`) moves the binding.

No result-accept function exists in 0001–0020. So "task succeeded, goal checking, binding running" is how every finished brief rests, not a stuck runtime. It still fails P3 as written: the new unit never receives these bindings, and after cutover a Hold, Stop or Resume on them routes to the old instance, which nobody polls.

**The permitted handler.** `POST /api/v1/projects/:projectId/commands` with `{kind:'stop', goalId, expectedGoalRevision, expectedAuthorityEpoch}`, admitted by `admit_goal_command` as a project editor. It is what the Studio's **Stop** button in the Work view sends. The dispatch and settlement are all existing functions:
1. The command writes one `native.stop` cleanup row per unsettled binding.
2. `dispatch_runtime_outbox` enqueues it to **the binding's own unit's** active instance, `s1-03-dev`.
3. The old bridge fences the attempt and answers `checked`.
4. `apply_runtime_receipt` sets the binding to `settled`.
5. `settle_native_control` sets the goal to `stopped` and the attempt to `stopped`, and cancels only `pending` and `running` jobs.

**Rehearsed** ([g5-preflight §1](../../evidence/SMC-M02/g5-preflight/README.md)). On both units, against the real API, PostgreSQL and worker, starting from exactly that resting state, a Stop gives:
- binding `settled`;
- job still `succeeded`;
- the same result source and revision, readable through the member route with its provider and model;
- `stop:checked`;
- no model call;
- P3's three counts at **0, 0, 0**.

A **Hold** gives `idle` and leaves P3 at 1. It does not settle.

**What it changes, for Davide to accept.** Each goal becomes **Stopped**, which is terminal, and the task's label goes from "Brief ready" to "Stopped". The brief stays readable ("Read the brief"). Nothing is accepted, published or deleted. The only alternative that keeps the goals open is to not cut over. A Hold strands them on a unit nobody runs.

**Not permitted, and not proposed:**
- rebinding the bindings to the new unit;
- SQL on bindings or goals;
- revoking the old instance before acceptance.
