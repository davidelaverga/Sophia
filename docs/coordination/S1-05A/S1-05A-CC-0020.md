## What the review found

The two fixes were right in themselves; the problem was what a three-minute queue does to the code around it. Google streams a reply faster than it plays, so its turn ends long before the room has heard the reply. Several paths still treated the end of Google's turn as the end of the reply.

| # | Finding | Fix in `f31cacd` |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Talking over a finished reply could not stop it. Google sends no `interrupted` for a turn it has ended, so up to 3 min kept playing | The holder's words over a reply still playing (sound after the turn ended, then Google's transcript) cut it, as Google's barge-in would. A late transcript with no new sound cuts nothing |
| 2 | A handoff after the turn ended let the old holder's reply play over the new holder | When the settle ends, whatever of the old reply is still playing is cut. The settle is marked at the handoff, so a turn ending before the next 100 ms tick is not missed |
| 3 | A GoAway cut a reply Google had already finished sending | A finished reply plays out; one cut off mid-turn still stops |
| 4 | Past the bound, frames were refused only while the queue was full, so the end of an overlong reply played with holes | Once a turn overflows, the rest of that turn is refused: a clean end |
| 5 | A turn's last partial frame waited, and was spliced onto the start of the next reply | Padded with silence and queued when the turn ends |
| 6 | A turn that began before the last one had played out left a stale "complete" flag, and could log early | It joins the same record, which now carries `turns` |
| 7 | Closing a session mid-reply logged nothing | Logged with `ended: closed` |
| 8 | A failed `left` or `gone` was retried on every 100 ms tick | Retried after 2 s |
| 9 | A blip in the bridge's own room link discarded a reported absence and its timer, so a flapping link never cleared the floor | The absence and its grace survive the blip. A holder still awaited is given the grace only for the time the link was up |
| 10 | A new holder who left before the next tick got the 5 s arrival grace instead of an immediate pause | The session notes who is present when the floor moves |
| 11 | "… is not in the room" was false once the holder came back while the exchange stayed paused | "Sophia paused: &lt;name&gt; was not in the room", matching the activity label |
| 12–14 | No unit test for the reply tally, a lost `clear()` assertion, a hard-coded frame duration, holder event plumbing | Tests added; duration derived; the absence passed through |

**Tests:** 15 new cases. Sixteen mutations, each reverting one fix, were each caught.

**Log changes for the runbook:** `audio.reply` gains `turns` and the ending `closed`, and `interrupted` now also covers the bridge's own barge-in. A reply that played whole has `playedMs` at least `receivedMs`, since its last frame is padded to 20 ms.

**Known limits:**
- The bridge's barge-in waits for Google's transcript of the holder's words. If Google begins its answer before that transcript arrives, the answer plays after the rest of the old reply. Stop Speaking still cuts at once.
- As with Google's own barge-in, an echo of Sophia's voice in the holder's microphone can cut her. With headphones it cannot.
