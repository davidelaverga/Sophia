## Root cause

- **How the queue behaved.** `OutputFramer` held at most 100 frames of 20 ms (2 s), and dropped the **oldest** unplayed frames beyond that, counted but never logged. Gemini Live streams a reply faster than the room plays it, so any reply more than 2 s ahead of playback lost its middle.
- **Reproduced.** The test room now holds playback as a full AudioSource does. With the deployed framer, a 10 s burst played 101 of 500 frames.

## Fix

- **The backlog is three minutes.** Past it, the newest frames are refused, so a reply loses its end, never its middle.
- **Every cut still clears the queue at once:** Stop Speaking, pause, handoff, barge-in and reconnect.
- **A new `audio.reply` log line, one per reply and content-free:**
  - `ended`;
  - `receivedMs` and `arrivalMs`;
  - `playedMs`;
  - `droppedMs` and `clearedMs`;
  - `maxQueuedMs`.

Tests and mutation checks are listed in the posted message.
