## What Codex found, and the fixes

| Finding | Fix in `44e68c2` |
|---|---|
| **P1: exchange start vs. guest token.** `request_guest_quiesce` can commit seeing no exchange; then an exchange opens before the guest's token is minted, and it was never quiesced for them | The gap is wider: the guest connects seconds after the mint, and a Resume in that gap has the same effect. **0015** stamps the token request in the transaction that pauses any live exchange, under the project lock that opening and resuming also take. For two minutes after it (the presence check's own freshness), opening and resuming refuse, as for a guest who is present |
| **P1: attribution after a turn.** A tool call after `turnComplete`, before the holder is heard again, was acted on as the previous speaker | A turn that ended takes its speaker with it; such a call is answered with a question. A lost connection keeps the speaker, so a call Google repeats on a resumed connection is still admitted once (A14) |
| **P2: "answering" after a stop.** Stop Speaking left the output reading `responding` until Google ended the stopped turn | The output reads idle at once. The fence still drops the turn, and no notice is sent while it may still arrive |
