## The invariant, and where it holds

**Invariant.** While a guest may hold a live room token, opening or resuming Sophia in that room is refused. That is, the fence's end is at or after the token's expiry.

- **The fence.** `room_lobby.guest_token_at` is stamped twice, each time under the project lock that opening and resuming also take:
  - when the guest asks, in `request_guest_quiesce`, which covers the wait before the mint;
  - again just before the mint, in the new `guest_token_minting()`. That function also re-checks the admission, so a decline is either seen there or comes after the stamp, where the removal watch takes over.
- **The window.** `guest_joining()` refuses for 630 s after the latest stamp: the token's 600 s from its mint (`ROOM_TOKEN_TTL_SECONDS`), plus 30 s for the step from the stamp to the mint and for clock differences between the database, the API and LiveKit. The API names the window `GUEST_FENCE_SECONDS`, and the crossing test pins the two together.
- **With the API at `e4d2e13` or later**, the room-token route calls `guest_token_minting()` and mints right after it, on every path. The fence then runs from the mint, whatever the wait, which closes CX-0047's gap exactly.
- **With the live API at `ce8bb8f`** (M1 alone), only the request is stamped. The fence runs 630 s from the request, and that API mints within its quiesce wait (5 s at most) plus one LiveKit presence call, which has no timeout. The bound therefore holds unless that call takes more than about 25 s. It becomes exact when the API at `e4d2e13` is deployed. That deploy is not requested here: it waits for Davide's review round, with OP-0006.

**Tests at `e4d2e13`:**
- The crossing asserts both that the fence's end is at or after the minted token's `expiresAt`, and that the mint's stamp comes after the quiesce's.
- A mint after a long wait restarts the fence.
- A declined guest's mint is refused and stamps nothing.
- The boundary sits at 625 s (refused) and 631 s (allowed).
- Three mutations were each caught: no stamp at the mint, a 600 s window, and a read-only re-check before the mint.
