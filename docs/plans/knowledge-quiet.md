# Knowledge, third pass: a quieter tile (K3)

> 2026-10-07 · After K2 (`docs/plans/knowledge-filters.md`), Luis: «Sigue». What the K2 captures left.

## The gap (measured, 1440 px, `?demo=1`, six tiles)

- Every tile ends in three lines of controls and credit: «Description by Sophia», «Edit», «History and changes», two
  of them underlined links. Across six tiles, eighteen lines of the same words.
- More reports is a bar the width of the view, heavier than the tiles it brings.

## What changes

- **One foot per tile:** History on the left, Edit on the right, on one quiet line in the second ink (4.5:1 or more), underlined only
  under the pointer or the focus. History is still named «History and changes» for a screen reader (its visible word
  is in its name).
- **Sophia's credit as her mark:** a description Sophia wrote opens with her mark, which says «Description by Sophia»
  to a screen reader and under the pointer. A member's edit still says so in words, «Edited by a member · Oct 3»: it
  is the exception, so it is written.
- **More reports, a button:** as wide as its words, in the middle under the tiles.

Nothing else moves: the cover, the meta line, the four type sizes, the presses over the tile.

## Checks (written first)

- A tile's History and Edit sit on one line, with no underline at rest.
- A description by Sophia shows no «Description by Sophia» in words, yet its accessible text says so; a member's edit
  is said in words.
- More reports is narrower than a tile and centred under the tiles.
- The tiles keep to the four type sizes; the existing Knowledge checks pass (the description edit and its focus,
  CX-0026, History from a tile).
