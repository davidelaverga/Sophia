# Conversations: decide here (C7)

> 2026-10-08 · The second of C6–C9 after Luis's «¿vale 30 dólares?» look at Conversations. Builds on
> `conversations-answers.md` (C6). Uses the brief's own writes (A08: `POST …/mission/proposals`,
> `POST …/mission/proposals/{id}/decision`), so it works against the real API, not only the demo. No API change.

## What reads as unfinished

- A conversation ends where its value starts: a point everyone agrees on can't become a decision without leaving for
  the brief and writing it again.
- The context shows what is «Still open» («Map first, list second · Proposed, not decided») and offers nothing to do
  about it: a decision waiting, and the person who can make it is looking right at it.

## What changes

- **Still open, decided where it's read.** In the context, each proposal waiting says who proposed it and when, and to
  an editor or admin offers «Accept» and «Not now» (A08's decide, at the revision they saw). Accepted, it joins the
  accepted decisions at the top; turned down, it leaves. If someone decided first, the context says so and shows the
  brief as it is now. Every member sees the same list; only those who can decide see the presses.
- **Propose from a message.** A message (a member's or Sophia's), pointed at or focused, offers «Propose as decision»:
  a short form under it, its words already in (cut to a sentence or two), to edit and send as a constraint (A08's
  propose). Sent, it shows in «Still open» beside the thread. Members who can write see it; viewers don't.
- **One brief everywhere.** What is proposed or decided here is the brief's: the room's brief and Updates read the same.

## Checks (written first)

- An editor accepts «Map first, list second» in the context: it shows among the accepted decisions, the write carries
  the revision read; «Not now» removes it. A viewer sees the proposal and no press.
- A decision answered with a stale revision says so and shows the brief again.
- «Propose as decision» on Marco's message opens the form with his words; sent, «Still open» lists it; the write is a
  constraint with that statement.
- The presses keep to the app's sizes and read at 4.5:1; the form takes the focus, Esc closes it and gives the focus back.
