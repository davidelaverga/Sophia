### Payer per meter

- Search and read keep the project's payer and caps.

### Fences

Current authority is required, never optional:

1. **Admission.** Grant, pin revision, requester eligibility, owner membership (source disclosure), connection, route
   qualification, runtime and account slot. The account lock serializes every project and alias.
2. **Every new paid model reservation.** Grant state and revision, both memberships (rows locked), credential revision,
   runtime link, qualification, and the three caps.
3. **Publication.** Requester, owner, grant revoked or admin-disabled, source withdrawal. Otherwise the result is
   **withheld**: the job fails `withheld: …`, the draft is kept as evidence, and a replay returns `withheld`. After a
   pause, expiry, revision change or rotation an already-authorized result **publishes** under its original payer and
   revision (`block_new_calls`, r2).
4. **Queued deliveries.** Denied at dispatch under current authority (r2: `runtime_unavailable` and
   `defer_native_delivery`, time-only expiry and qualification included), and also by the change that makes them
   ineligible: a grant, connection, runtime-link or membership change.

There is no fallback payer, and unpinned legacy behaviour is unchanged. Every legacy picker excludes contribution
runtimes.
