## Behaviour: the established tolerant reader

- **Read:** canonical owner and teammate pages (`mine: null` for a teammate), and every A51 enum value for tool,
  resource state, connection state, host state, qualification state, operations and `notReported`. Also the nullable
  times and host ages, empty and 200-row pages, and `nextCursor` as a UUID or null.
- **Refused (`ContractViolation`):**
  - a missing required field, including nullable-but-required ones such as `nextCursor`, `mine` and `host`'s fields;
  - a wrong type or an unknown enum value;
  - an id or cursor that is not a UUID, a time that is not a `date-time`, or an impossible date;
  - a label over 200 characters;
  - a host age that is negative, fractional or over the safe-integer bound;
  - 201 resources, 4 operations or 9 `notReported` entries;
  - a null `recordedQualification`.
- **Dropped, not refused:** a property the contract does not list, at every depth, in place on the input.
- **Not promised:** relational meaning. A consumer must still judge `isYou` against `mine`, operations as an ordered
  set, `nextCursor` against `complete`, and qualification state against its times; the tests show such shapes parse.

**Correction to the earlier Resources adapter proposal:** it said the canonical parser would reject unknown extra
fields. It does not. Like every reader here, it drops them (`docs/plans/tolerant-readers.md`). The global compiler
was not changed. The adapter must not rely on extra-field rejection, and must make the relational checks itself.
