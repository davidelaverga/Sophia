mark, three or more, mismatched families, rarer glyphs — is `malformed_quotation` and the whole input abstains,
adoption included. A quote mark after a backslash, or a quote written as an escape or entity (`\u0027`, `\x27`,
`&#39;`, `&apos;`, `&quot;` …), is unsupported escaping and abstains; `‟ 〝 〞 ＂` anywhere abstain. A conservative
lexical fallback, not a parser: whatever character precedes or follows a mark no longer decides authority.

input abstain even when nothing is quoted; three r3/r4 tests that expected them to stay ordinary were changed to

outside one exact pair stay meaningful.
