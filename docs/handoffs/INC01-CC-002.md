## Outcome

One consumed, durable path, behind flags that stay off:

1. An owner admits an operation on their own qualified native resource. This is `POST
   /api/v1/projects/{id}/runner-operations` (A47), calling `sophia.admit_owner_runner_operation` (0092). The
   operation, its command and its outbox row are written in one transaction, anchored on `executor_resources`,
   `execution_connections`, `goals`, `work_attempts`, `execution_bindings`, `commands` and `outbox`.
2. The worker's `runnerDispatchOnce` claims one owner runner's rows. Prepare-send (`owner_runner_prepare_send`)
   rechecks current authority and commits the possible send before the gateway is called.
3. The owner gateway (`RunnerGateway`) checks its own profile and journals the intent, the native identities derived
   under the current namespace, and the possible send (fsynced) before it makes the one mapped T3 call.
4. Lost answers are reconciled by the original identities and never resent. Received operations are observed until
   they settle, and the account reservation is held until then.
5. The owner reads a truthful retained status (`GET …/runner-operations[/{operationId}]`). Received is never shown as
   accepted, applied or verified.

Exercised only through `FakeT3`, on real PostgreSQL 16 with RLS and a real disk journal. This is not the INC-01 live
connection or app outcome. The next mandatory assignment is INC01-CC-003.

- **F01 (P1). A Stop settled over an input still in transit.** A Stop now settles, and ends its binding, only when
  both hold:
  - no input of its binding is `admitted`, `possible_send` or `unknown`;
  - a fresh observation proves no active run, and it was made under the inputs' current barrier token. The token
    changes when an input converges.

  The rest of the repair:
  - **No cached settlement.** A gateway's journaled `settled` is a cached past read: SQL settles nothing on it, and
    the dispatcher answers it with a fresh observation.
  - **A late landing.** An input that lands after the Stop was sent marks that Stop overtaken. The owner may then Stop
    again: a new Stop with every existing check, also with admissions or sends off. Its interrupt reaches the late run.
  - **Never.** Nothing replays work, releases a reservation or claims a stronger T3 guarantee. At the pin,
    `thread.stop` does not refuse a later request. An input that stays unknown keeps the Stop unresolved, for an
    operator.
- **F02 (P2). A duplicate Stop under a fresh key was an unbound success.**
  - It is now refused (`invalid_state`, naming the pending Stop), unless the pending Stop was overtaken. A refusal
    writes nothing, so the refused key binds nothing.
  - The same key replays its own receipt, before and after settlement.
  - Concurrent gestures give one Stop and one interrupt.
- **F03 (P2). Maintenance locked rows before the project.**
  - It now chooses candidates without locks, then locks each one's project, then its operation and outbox rows, all
    `SKIP LOCKED`. It rechecks the candidate under those locks.
  - It never waits, so it deadlocks neither with a recorder nor with another maintainer.
