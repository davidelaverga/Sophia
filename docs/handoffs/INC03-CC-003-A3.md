## Outcome

Plan: [docs/plans/personal-answers.md](../plans/personal-answers.md), section "A3", written before any product code.

### Control is its own state (C6, C7, seq 100)
- `personal_answer_controls` holds a revision from 0 to 1000; no row means active, revision 0. It is independent of 0101's decisions, so an input admitted at decision seq 100 is controllable.
- No A53 decision is appended or reinterpreted.
- **Closed transitions:**
  - C1: cancel an admitted input;
  - C2: replace it (the original is withdrawn and a new correlated input is kept);
  - C3: admit a held replacement, or C3′ refuse it with the refusal remembered;
  - C4: cancel a held replacement.
- **Identity and replay:** every operation names its key, epoch and expected revision. The epoch fence and the key come first; a replay returns its remembered receipt, a refused admission included, before any flag or profile is read, and wakes nothing.

### The launch fence (C1, C2)
**Controlled reservation.** `reserve_personal_answer_controlled` wraps 0102's unchanged guard. In the same transaction it tags a reserved call with:
- its frozen execution snapshot and digest;
- the input's control revision.

**Launch intent.** `begin_personal_answer_launch(call, claim, digest)` is the one-shot intent.
- It rechecks the claim, epoch, control, latest admission, frozen profile against the execution, and the payer's enrolment, with the payer row held to COMMIT (space before payer).
- Only a fresh `granted` lets the call prepare.
- A replayed grant (`already_launched`), `withdrawn`, `refused` or `not_tagged` never does.
- A lost acknowledgement prepares nothing and leaves the call possibly launched.

**On the live companion's side.**
- It remembers its protocol from the reservation. Only a positively absent 0103 (marker missing, read without error) takes b760's path.
- After the grant, a failure, mismatch or stop during preparation is held uncertain (`prepare_unknown`, `stopped_after_launch`), never released. Only a stop seen before preparation is invoked is released.

### Withdrawn work and late results (C3)
- A withdrawn input's turn leaves `pending`/`failed` for `withdrawn`; an answered one stays answered.
- A kept answer moves to `personal_withheld_results`.
- **The settlement**, replaced under its own signature, grants, SECURITY DEFINER and search path, retains a late body of the original call:
  - whether the turn is `withdrawn` or `answered`, and even when the cost was already settled or reconciled;
  - its financial status follows 0073's own rule;
  - an identical repeat is idempotent; a different one is refused, never overwritten.
- The live companion recognizes its own withheld answer after a lost settlement acknowledgement.
- No plain, kept, fallback or suggestion path publishes withdrawn work.

### Activity and context (C4)
- `ACTIVITY` (b760's `NOT_HELD`, equal to 0099) is used for welcome-due. 0099 is unchanged.
- `PROMPT_ELIGIBLE` also excludes a withdrawn input and every reply to it, before LIMIT.
- A never-admitted replacement, held or cancelled, stays physically `recorded`.

### Owner projection (C5, A54)
- **New routes:** control, admit, the input view, the log, the controlled turn pages, and the versioned export.
- **Old unversioned reads** (space, poll, earlier, export) answer `503 projection_unavailable` to an owner with any controlled input, an answered one and another pending turn included.
- A10, A52 and A53 are byte-unchanged.

### Availability (C6)
- Installation, a positively complete 0103, is the boundary. Absent: 503 `projection_unavailable`. A read error: 503 `unavailable`.
- There is no controls flag.
- Paid admission of a replacement needs `personalAnswers` AND `personalAnswerReplacementAdmission` (default off; `server.ts` unchanged) and the live profile.
