## Acceptance evidence

- An external team repeatedly creates, directs and reviews useful work through Sophia.
- A human engineer can continue the project from the delivered source and records.
- Legacy retirement preserves required data meaning and completes cleanup responsibilities.

## Adverse checks

- Fixture-only usage is not counted as customer adoption.
- A failed migration does not mutate the live legacy source.
- A partial export clearly reports omitted private or inaccessible material.
