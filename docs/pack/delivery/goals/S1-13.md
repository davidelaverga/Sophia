## Acceptance evidence

- One substantive report and one deck are generated from retained source and readable in the app.
- The files open correctly and include the intended images and sources.
- The normal memory/authority route works; no special bypass admission is used.

## Adverse checks

- A renderer failure preserves prior output and returns diagnostic evidence.
- The small legacy PDF smoke wrapper is not presented as a complete rich renderer.
- An editable-source claim is not made for flattened raster output.
